## Limits and migration

All checks were background/offline or used a hidden standalone test process. No live CS2 launch/injection, in-demo composition test, ReShade coexistence test or FPS measurement was performed. Automatic map collision avoidance is not implemented; the eye-origin fix prevents the confirmed incorrect world-origin placement rather than solving all physical wall intersections.

Framing v6 backs up old settings before resetting muzzle-relative player trim once; prop settings remain. A pawn destroyed/recreated across respawn/demo seek requires a new lock. Restart HLAE/CS2 after replacing a previously loaded DLL. The formal release classification does not imply zero crashes or zero FPS loss.
